Our Story,Our Flavour

Our Story, Our Flavour

A.I. Foods started from one simple love: mama food. We wanted a place where you fit eat real Nigerian food — no stress, no long wait, just fresh food wey go make you smile. Every morning, we cook fresh. No yesterday food. From our amala + ewedu + gbegiri, to our smoky jollof rice with chicken, fried rice with turkey, ofada with ayamase, pounded yam with egusi, and pepper soup with catfish — everything is made like home. We no dey rush our food. We cook am with patience, with fresh pepper, fresh ingredients, and plenty love. Na why our ewedu dey draw well, our amala dey soft, our jollof dey smoky, and our soups dey rich and tasty. Whether na family lunch, date night, birthday, or you just dey crave something tasty after long day — your table dey wait for you. You fit sit down and enjoy for our restaurant, or order on WhatsApp and we go deliver sharp sharp to your door. At A.I. Foods, you no be just customer. You be family.
